Our Process

How a House Gets Built

Ten stages, in the order they actually happen. Keep scrolling to walk the job from the first drawing to the front door key.

  1. 01

    Design & Drawings

    We sit down with you, an architect, and an engineer where the job needs one, and settle the layout, the structure, and the budget on paper. Decisions are cheap at this stage.

  2. 02

    Permits & Approvals

    We pull the permits and handle the township, the inspectors, and the paperwork. You do not chase any of it.

  3. 03

    Site Work & Excavation

    Machines show up and the ground opens. We clear the lot, set the elevations, and dig for the footings, watching drainage before anything gets poured.

  4. 04

    Footings & Foundation

    Forms, rebar, and concrete. Square, level, drained, and waterproofed, because every mistake down here gets more expensive with each floor added on top of it.

  5. 05

    Framing & Structure

    Walls, floors, and engineered trusses go up and the building finally has a shape. Straight walls and correct openings here save every trade that follows.

  6. 06

    Roof & Exterior

    Roof, windows, doors, and siding close the building in. Once it is weather-tight, the weather stops setting your schedule.

  7. 07

    Rough-In: Plumbing, Electric, HVAC

    Pipes, wire, and ductwork go in while the walls are still open, then get inspected. This is the part nobody sees and everybody lives with.

  8. 08

    Insulation & Drywall

    The house gets sealed, insulated, hung, taped, and sanded. Flat walls and clean corners are what make the finish work look right.

  9. 09

    Finishes

    Cabinets, counters, tile, flooring, trim, fixtures, and paint. This is the part you touch every day, so it gets the most attention.

  10. 10

    Walkthrough & Handover

    We walk the finished job with you, fix the punch list, hand over the documents and warranties, and you have our number afterwards.

Services Overview

01

Major Projects

Home Renovations

A good renovation makes the house work better without stripping out the reason you bought it.

We take on whole-house renovations: multiple rooms, structural changes, new layouts, updated mechanical systems, and finishes.

Every trade and every phase runs through us, so there is one person to call from planning through the final walkthrough.

What you end up with reads as one house, not a stack of projects done years apart by different people.

Whole-house renovations, planned before anyone swings a hammer.

Home Additions

Sometimes the house is right and there just isn't enough of it.

We design and build additions that add room and value while looking like they were part of the original house.

Bedrooms, a bigger kitchen, a family room, a second floor, or a serious expansion of the footprint: we look at the whole property, not only the new square footage.

Structure, rooflines, interior flow, mechanicals, daylight, and finishes get planned together. That is what keeps the seam between old and new from showing.

New Construction

Build from the ground up and you get to make every decision correctly the first time.

We run new residential construction from early planning and site prep through framing, systems, finishes, and final completion.

Architects, engineers, designers, subs, suppliers, owners: we keep all of them pointed at the same schedule and hold them to it.

Build it right, answer the phone, and hand over a house we are willing to put our name on.

Large-Scale Home Remodels

On a big remodel, craftsmanship is the easy part. Coordination is what goes wrong.

When structural work, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, framing, millwork, flooring, finishes, and design are all live at once, one missed conversation turns into a change order.

We run complicated residential remodels as a single construction process instead of a pile of separate jobs.

Because we can see the whole project, a decision made in framing still works when the tile goes down. That is what keeps delays, rework, and surprise costs off your bill.

Basement Build-Outs & Finishing

Finishing a basement is the cheapest usable square footage you will ever add to a house.

We turn unfinished lower levels into space that feels like part of the house: bedrooms, family rooms, offices, gyms, playrooms, and full or half baths.

Basements come with problems the upstairs doesn't have, so we deal with those first: moisture and waterproofing, insulation, ceiling height, egress, moving mechanicals and utilities, and code.

Once it is dry and sound, it gets finished to the same standard as any other floor.

Structure, Concrete & Framing

Concrete & Foundation Work

Everything above the foundation is only as good as the foundation.

We do concrete and foundation work: excavation coordination, footings, foundation walls, slabs, basement floors, garage pads, and exterior flatwork.

Elevations, drainage, waterproofing, rebar, and backfill are part of the same scope, not somebody else's problem later. Mistakes down here are the most expensive ones to fix.

Square, level, drained, and ready for whatever goes on top.

Framing & Structural Carpentry

Every trade after framing inherits whatever the framers did.

We frame new construction, additions, and remodels, and we do structural alterations to existing buildings: load-bearing changes, beams, headers, and new openings.

We handle engineered floor and roof truss packages start to finish, from layout and ordering through delivery and setting, so the package shows up correct and goes up on schedule.

Straight walls, right dimensions, correct rough openings. Nobody downstream should have to fix our work.

Get the structure right and every trade after it goes easier.

Commercial Framing

Commercial work runs on speed, organization, and being where you said you would be.

We frame commercial projects, hit the schedule, communicate, and stay out of the way of the other trades on site.

Commercial Construction

Commercial projects take a different level of coordination.

Trades, changing conditions, deadlines, inspections, deliveries, drawings, revisions, and client requests all move at once, and all of it has to stay straight.

We pair experienced construction management with software we built ourselves, which gives the team a clearer view of what is happening on every active job.

The software supports good construction. It does not replace it.

Progress, open items, documentation, and the details that get forgotten are all tracked in one place, so the crew knows what is done and what is next.

It is simply how we work, and it costs our clients nothing extra.

Better information on site means fewer problems in the building.

05

Multifamily Construction & Renovation

A multifamily property has to show well on day one and still hold up five years of tenants later.

We build and renovate multifamily with durability, usable space, privacy, and maintenance costs in mind.

Rentals take far more abuse than a private home. Materials, fixtures, surfaces, layouts, and methods have to be chosen for how tenants really live.

That is no excuse for units that look institutional.

We aim for units that rent well, are cheap to maintain, resist the damage that normally happens anyway, and use every foot of space without putting bedrooms against each other.

For an owner or developer, the number that matters is not the construction cost.

We build for the life of the property.

06

Repairs & Property Maintenance

Owning property is not one construction project. It is a standing obligation.

We handle repairs and maintenance for landlords, property managers, and organizations running several buildings at once.

That covers turnovers between tenants, plumbing and electrical repairs, drywall and finish work, flooring, doors and hardware, carpentry, bathroom and kitchen fixes, exterior work, and the recurring items that keep a building in shape and in compliance.

Running a portfolio takes more than a one-time contractor. You need somebody who picks up the phone, shows up, already knows the building, writes down what was done, and can take a call today without a whole introduction.

We built the repair side of the business around exactly that, and we cover organizations with recurring work across many locations, including residential group homes such as those run by Keystone Human Services.

One contractor, every property, same standard.

07

Institutional & Public Bid Work

Public and institutional work gets held to a higher standard, and it should be.

We bid institutional and public work, including projects issued through PennBid, county and municipal vendor lists, and architect-managed bid lists.

Price is the smallest part of it. These jobs want complete and responsive bids, prevailing wage and documentation compliance where it applies, insurance and bonding, firm schedules, inspection sequences, and reporting the whole way through.

We treat all of that as part of the work instead of a nuisance. Our project management and documentation are set up to produce the paper trail these jobs require while the actual construction stays on schedule.

For an agency, a municipality, an institution, or the architect running the job, that means a contractor who is easy to award, easy to administer, and still answering at closeout.

Qualified, responsive, and still accountable at closeout.

Make the decision while it is still cheap to make.
